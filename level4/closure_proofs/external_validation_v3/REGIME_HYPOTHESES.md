# Regime hypotheses

All interpretations in this file are **POST-HOC HYPOTHESES** derived from the
already final V2 campaign. They may motivate coverage but may not predict or
select V3 success from pilot reuse-policy results.

- **Dependence hypothesis:** P2 non-inferiority may be easier to establish when
  the predictive residual is close to serially white.
- **tail hypothesis:** heavy tails may amplify uncertainty in simultaneous
  intervention inference even when reference distortion is present.
- **adaptation-direction hypothesis:** in strongly seasonal/persistent streams,
  full reuse may lower alert burden by adapting toward alarm-selected states,
  so distortion and operational penalty can separate.
- **power hypothesis:** a 40-block floor may distinguish a real safety failure
  from V2's borderline simultaneous intervals more reliably than 20 blocks.
- **non-explanations retained:** the V2 threshold, train residual scale, and
  evaluation-length/ARL ratio did not align monotonically with joint support.

Selected coverage is deliberately contrasting: MetroPT-3 has persistent,
heavy-tailed pilot calibration residuals, while Online Retail II has near-white
but heavy-tailed pilot calibration residuals. No P0/P1/P2 result was generated
or inspected during this choice.
