# R-A method-repair feasibility audit

Written **before** any R-A implementation exists and before any R-A number. It
answers one question: does panel-local Taylor expansion of `phi` address both
mechanisms measured in `../STOP_GATE.md` §4, and by how much?

The first certified method **failed**. That is a fact of the record, not a
debugging step, and nothing below reinterprets it. R-A was identified only
*after* that failure, from its diagnosis.

---

## 1. The two measured mechanisms, restated exactly

The failed method replaced `phi` by one **global Maclaurin polynomial about
`0`** of degree `2n = 100`, with the uniform Lagrange remainder

```text
eps_Mac(n, zeta_max) = (zeta_max^2 / 2)^{n+1} / ( (n+1)! sqrt(2 pi) ) ,
```

valid on `|zeta| <= zeta_max`.

**M1 — truncation blow-up with drift.** In the failed method the expansion
argument was the *shifted* innovation, so `zeta_max = c_D + |e|` and

```text
eps_Mac(50, c_D + |e|) / eps_Mac(50, c_D) = ( (c_D+|e|) / c_D )^{2(n+1)} = ( (c_D+|e|)/c_D )^{102} .
```

| `|e|` | predicted ratio | measured ratio |
|---|---|---|
| `0.26` | `(5.76/5.5)^102 = 111.2` | `4.17665e-5 / 3.75603e-7 = 111.2` |
| `12` | `(17.5/5.5)^102 = 1.87e51` | `7.04071e+44 / 3.75603e-7 = 1.874e51` |

The mechanism is understood to two significant figures.

**M2 — interval dependency.** Measured amplification `4.96e41` per unit
`e`-ball radius, exactly linear from `1e-8` to `1e-2`. Its seed is the
conditioning of the same global expansion: at `u = zeta^2/2 = 16.59` the series
`sum_n (-u)^n / n!` has terms peaking at `n ~ u` with magnitude
`e^u / sqrt(2 pi u) ~ 1.6e6`, summing to `e^{-16.59} = 6.2e-8` — an intrinsic
cancellation of `~2.6e13` — and the degree-`~102` powers of the affine limits
then carry that into the power-basis and Bernstein conversions.

## 2. R-A as literally sketched does not compose with this architecture

`../STOP_GATE.md` §8 sketched "panel-local Taylor expansions on a subdivision of
the innovation range". Taken literally there are two variants, and both fail
structurally — this must be said before anything is built.

**Variant (i): state-independent panel boundaries.** The symbolic pipeline
integrates over the continuation interval `(l(x), u(x))` with the reset kinks
`alpha = 1/2 - p`, `beta = m - 1/2` inside it. All four are **affine in the
state**. A fixed grid of `N` constant panel edges therefore interleaves with
them in an order that varies over the state domain, so the `(p, m)` domain must
be partitioned by `~N` further affine cuts into `O(N^2)` subregions, each
needing its own reachable-set parameterisation and Bernstein cover. That is a
redesign of `_max_abs_on_reachable`, not a change of expansion.

**Variant (ii): panels that follow the limits.** Then each panel centre `c` is
affine in `(p, m)`, and the expansion coefficients are `phi^{(k)}(c)/k!`, which
are **transcendental in `(p, m)`** — they are exactly the objects the whole
polynomial architecture exists to avoid. The residual would no longer be a
polynomial and the Bernstein continuum bound would not apply.

**Conclusion.** R-A's literal form is not implementable here. Its *intent* —
make the expansion error locally small, and keep the drift out of the
ill-conditioned chain — is implementable, by two devices derived below. The
repaired method is named **R-A′** to keep the distinction honest.

## 3. Device 1 — recentring, which eliminates M1 exactly (not merely reduces it)

The failed method expanded `phi` about `0` in the *shifted* argument. Expand it
instead about the drift itself. Writing `z` for the **unshifted** innovation
(the frozen model's own variable, in which the continuation interval
`(l, u) = (m - 11/2, 11/2 - p)`, the kinks and the state map `q(x, z)` contain
no `e` at all), the exact Taylor expansion of the weight about `e` is

```text
phi(z + e) = sum_{i>=0} b_i(e) z^i ,     b_i(e) = phi^{(i)}(e) / i!
                                                = (-1)^i He_i(e) phi(e) / i! ,
```

with `He_i` the probabilists' Hermite polynomials, generated by the exact,
numerically stable recurrence `He_0 = 1`, `He_1 = e`,
`He_{i+1}(e) = e He_i(e) - i He_{i-1}(e)`. For rational `e` every `He_i(e)` is
rational; no cancellation-prone binomial double sum is used.

**The expansion variable is `z`, and `|z| <= 11/2` for every state and every
`e`.** Truncating at degree `N` and using Cramér's inequality
`|He_n(x)| e^{-x^2/4} <= 1.086 sqrt(n!)`, hence
`|phi^{(n)}(x)| <= (1.086 / sqrt(2 pi)) sqrt(n!) = 0.4333 sqrt(n!)`, the
Lagrange remainder is

```text
eps_rec(N) = 0.4333 * (11/2)^{N+1} / sqrt( (N+1)! )      — independent of e .
```

The same recentring applies to the reward `rho_{1,e}(x) = phi(u+e) - phi(l+e)
- e(1 - Phi(u+e) + Phi(l+e))`: its arguments `u + e = 11/2 + e - p` and
`l + e = m - 11/2 + e` are expanded about `3 + e` and `-3 + e`, whose expansion
variables `5/2 - p` and `m - 5/2` are again **e-free** and bounded by `5/2`.

| quantity | failed method (`n = 50`) | R-A′ (`N = 120`) |
|---|---|---|
| kernel truncation at `e = 0` | `3.756e-07` | `~6e-12` |
| kernel truncation at `|e| <= 0.26` | `4.177e-05` | `~6e-12` |
| kernel truncation at `e = 6.5` | `1.4e+28` | `~6e-12` |
| kernel truncation at `e = 12` | `7.041e+44` | `~6e-12` |
| reward truncation, any `e` | `O(eps_Mac)` | `<= 0.4333 (5/2)^{121}/sqrt(121!) ~ 1e-53` |

**Quantitative prediction for M1: eliminated uniformly in `e`.** The
contribution to `delta` becomes `(11 sup|ghat| + 2 + 2 e_hi (c_D + e_hi)) *
eps_rec(120) ~ 33 * 6e-12 = 2e-10`, i.e. `C * 2e-10 ~ 2.5e-7` in the enclosure —
five orders below the `1.5e-4` budget, at **every** drift including `e_far`.
This is the sense in which R-A′ is genuinely "panel-local": one expansion panel
per expansion site, centred on that site.

## 4. Device 2 — an exact-centre Taylor model in `e`, which removes M2 by construction

M2 is caused by feeding an interval-valued `e` into the symbolic chain. R-A′
never does. Instead, for a sub-cell `e in [e_0 - h, e_0 + h]` with `e_0` an
**exact rational**:

```text
g(x_0; e) = g(x_0; e_0) + (e - e_0) d_e g(x_0; e_0) + (1/2)(e - e_0)^2 d_e^2 g(x_0; xi)
R(e)      = e + g(x_0; e)                                        (PROOF.md L1.7, m = 1)
```

* `g(·; e_0)` is certified by the inherited pipeline at radius zero — the
  configuration that is already validated against the `Gamma` `a` equation;
* `d_e g(·; e_0)` is certified by the **differentiated Bellman equation**, which
  is again a single second-kind equation on the same square:

  ```text
  d_e g = K_e d_e g + (d_e K_e) g + d_e rho_{1,e} ,
  ```

  with `(d_e K_e)` obtained from the same kernel routine fed the derivative
  coefficients `d_e b_i = (i+1) b_{i+1}` (an exact consequence of
  `d_e phi(z+e) = phi'(z+e)`), and

  ```text
  d_e rho_{1,e}(x) = -(u+e) phi(u+e) + (l+e) phi(l+e)
                     - (1 - Phi(u+e) + Phi(l+e)) + e ( phi(u+e) - phi(l+e) ) ;
  ```

* `d_e^2 g` is **not** certified; it is bounded by a closed bootstrap (§5).

Every symbolic computation happens at an exact rational `e_0`, so the input
radius on `e` is exactly zero and the `4.96e41` amplification has nothing to
act on. **Quantitative prediction for M2: eliminated by construction**; the only
`e`-radius left is the `h` and `h^2` terms above, which are ordinary analysis,
not interval arithmetic.

## 5. The bootstrap, and the sub-cell width it forces

Differentiating `g = K_e g + rho_{1,e}` twice in `e` and taking sup norms over
the sub-cell, with
`a := ||d_e K||_inf <= 2 phi(0) = 0.7978845608`,
`b := ||d_e^2 K||_inf <= 4 phi(1) = 0.9678828981`,
`c_1 := sup|d_e rho_1| <= 1 + a|e|`,
`c_2 := sup|d_e^2 rho_1| <= 1.13788 + b|e|`
(the constant `1.13788 = int |y||y^2-1| phi(y) dy`), and
`C := ||(I - K_e)^{-1}||_inf`:

```text
S_2 <= C [ 2 a S_1 + b S_0 + c_2 ] ,   S_1 <= G_1 + h S_2 ,   S_0 <= G_0 + h S_1 ,
```

where `G_0 = sup|g(·;e_0)| <= sup|ghat| + C delta` and
`G_1 = sup|d_e g(·;e_0)| <= sup|ghat'| + C delta'` are **certified** at `e_0`.
Substituting closes iff `C (2 a h + b h^2) < 1`, i.e. essentially
`h < 1/(2 a C)`. R-A′ freezes

```text
h := 1 / (4 a C)      =>   closure factor 1/2,   S_2 <= 2 C [ 2 a G_1 + b G_0 + b h G_1 + c_2 ] .
```

With the already-certified `C = 1239.2722762545090` for this cell,
`h = 2.5283268e-4`, so the gate cell `[0.24, 0.26]` is covered by **40
sub-cells**, and with the anticipated `G_0 ~ 2.6`, `G_1 ~ 3`:
`S_2 <= 2.15e4` and the Taylor remainder is `(h^2/2) S_2 = 6.9e-4`.

**This is why the sub-cell width is a derived constant, not a tuning knob:** it
is fixed by `C` and the two universal Gaussian constants, and it is computed
before any residual is evaluated.

## 6. Quantitative prediction for the new stop-gate

Per sub-cell, the enclosure half-width of `R` is

```text
h  +  C delta  +  h ( |ghat'(x_0)| + C delta' )  +  (h^2 / 2) S_2 .
```

Using the already-measured value-equation residual at this drift
(`3.3355e-5`, from the failed method's own point-`e` diagnostic — a
representation-independent quantity), `C delta = 0.0414`; taking
`|ghat'(x_0)| ~ 2` and a derivative-equation residual `delta' ~ 1e-4`:

| term | predicted |
|---|---|
| sub-cell `e`-range `h` | `2.5e-4` |
| `C delta` (value equation) | `4.1e-2` |
| `h(|ghat'| + C delta')` | `5.4e-4` |
| `(h^2/2) S_2` | `6.9e-4` |
| spread of `R` across the gate cell (`|R'| ~ 0.08` near the maximum) | `8e-4` |
| **predicted gate half-width** | **`~0.043`** |

with an honest uncertainty band of **`0.03`–`0.09`**, dominated by the unknown
`delta'`. Against the frozen threshold `0.2` this predicts **PASS with a factor
of roughly `4`**. The prediction is recorded here, before the run, so that the
outcome can falsify it.

## 7. Cover feasibility beyond the gate cell (not part of the gate)

Device 1 makes the truncation `e`-independent, so the far field is no longer
excluded by truncation. What remains `e`-dependent is `C`, and it improves
sharply with drift — the drift-explicit block-forcing bound gives `5323` at
`e = 0`, `1239` on this cell, and exactly `1.000` at `e = 12`. Since
`h = 1/(4 a C)`, the sub-cell width **widens** as `|e|` grows, so the cover cost
is concentrated near the origin. That is a favourable structure, and it is the
opposite of the failed method's behaviour.

Two facts must be recorded against it: the number of sub-cells near `e = 0` is
large, and the cover's total cost is set there; and `FROZEN_SCOPE.md` §3's
`e_far = 12` is now unnecessarily far, since `P5X-T3` is proved and monotone
from `|e| >= c_D + 1 = 6.5` (CUSUM). Shortening the certified range to
`[0, 6.5]` would be a strict strengthening of the analytic side, but it deviates
from a frozen declaration and is therefore **flagged for adjudication, not
adopted**.
