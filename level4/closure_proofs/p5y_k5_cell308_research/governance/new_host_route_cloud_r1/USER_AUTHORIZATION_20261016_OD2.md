## Interpretation used (coordinator; for review)

* **Reviewer selection is delegated to the coordinator** by this instruction ("Select fresh, eligible independent
  reviewers"). The coordinator therefore appoints fresh Claude review agents, each in a new context with a written
  brief.
* The appointment is recorded in the ledger before substantive review. Each reviewer's eligibility and exposure
  disclosure is committed before its substantive review begins.
* **Stated limit:** these reviewers are agents launched by the coordinator in the coordinator's session. They are fresh
  and independent of the reviewed authorship, but they are **not** independent of the coordinator's environment or
  model family, and they are not human reviewers.
* This instruction does not satisfy S1, S16(c), §14 / G2, MBS-6 / MBS-7 / MBS-8, OD-1 or OD-3, and decides no D-*
  item.
