## The strength rule

```text
strength(v) = min( rank(class(v)),
                   min over LOGICAL_PREMISE parents p of strength(p),
                   4 if v has any ASSUMPTION edge )
```

`ASSUMPTION` caps at `CONDITIONAL_THEOREM` rather than at the assumption's own
rank, because declaring a hypothesis openly is exactly what a conditional
theorem is allowed to do. Every other edge type is excluded: a kernel check
*about* a theorem is not a premise *of* it, a measurement consistent with a
claim does not license it, and an adjudicated status is not evidence.

Rule `V15` requires `strength(v) >= rank(class(v))` for every claim. It holds
for all 57, and unlike P9's rank check it is not the only guard — `V1`, `V2`,
`V10` and `V11` constrain the *classes themselves* against their sources, which
is what P9's self-graded validator could not do.

## The collapsed-graph diagnostic

Running the same validator on a copy of the graph with every edge flattened to
`LOGICAL_PREMISE` yields **36 violations** against the typed graph's **0**. The
violations are concretely the unsound propagations a collapsed representation
would license: formal-support and certified-support edges become premises of
theorems; empirical support becomes a premise; adjudicated statuses become
premises; and `ASM-DOM`/`ASM-MONO` become premises of exact theorems. This is
the evidence that a dependency graph of this project drawn with untyped edges
is unsound. It is stored in `results/dependency_graph.json` under
`collapsed_diagnostic`.
