Coordinator: new binding rule S8, now appended to PREAMBLE.md.

Never place a tightness figure or improvement ratio next to, or combined with, committed TAIL-CELL factors, margins or critical values, whether in prose, tables or reasoning. This covers pairings such as "we achieve X% tightness, which would/would not settle 4.311 vs 4.4429". The 3.06% scale may appear only as the stated motivating scale, never as a pass/fail target or next to your achieved tightness as a verdict about 308. If anything you have already written does this, correct it and note the correction.
