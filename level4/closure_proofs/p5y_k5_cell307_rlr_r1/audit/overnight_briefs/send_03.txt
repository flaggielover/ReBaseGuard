Coordinator: new binding rule S8, now appended to PREAMBLE.md, after an incident in the campaign record.

Never place a new route's gain factor next to, or combined with, committed TAIL-CELL shares, factors or margins, whether in prose, tables, rankings or reasoning. This covers:
- a generic theorem factor;
- a synthetic or non-target improvement ratio;
- tail items such as the C5 radius shares for 306–309 (e.g. A0·ρ·f_G 58.8% at 309), C8 x-eff factors, C3 knockout values, the C5 oracle/lever percentages, and E10.

Such a product estimates the route's target effect, so it is a target-equivalent proxy. Committed history may be quoted only in a separate section with no route factor attached. If anything you have already written does this, correct it and note the correction.
