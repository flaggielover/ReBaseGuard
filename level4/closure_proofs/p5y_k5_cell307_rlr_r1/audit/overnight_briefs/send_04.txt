Coordinator: new binding rule S8, now appended to PREAMBLE.md, after an incident in the campaign record.

Never place a new route's gain factor next to, or combined with, committed TAIL-CELL constants, shares, factors or margins, whether in prose, tables, rankings or reasoning. This covers:
- LR-vs-Dv′ ratios from synthetic or non-target drifts;
- committed tail A1/A2 values, C3 knockouts and C8 x-eff factors.

Such a product estimates the route's target effect, so it is a target-equivalent proxy. Asymptotic orders are fine; tail numbers next to them are not. If anything you have already written does this, correct it and note the correction.
