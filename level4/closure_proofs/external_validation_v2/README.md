# External validation V2

This isolated campaign evaluates the ReBaseGuard operational mechanism on
three independently selected, sufficiently powered semi-real streams. It is a
later campaign, not a repair or reinterpretation of historical Stage E.

Authoritative boundaries:

- historical Stage E remains `STAGE-E-PARTIAL` with 0/3 H-E5 tasks;
- historical Stage F and the post-closure global re-audit remain
  `LEVEL-4-PARTIAL`;
- no Stage-E data or outcome is pooled into V2;
- no confirmatory V2 policy comparison may exist before the protocol hash;
- closure means semi-real mechanism support, not deployment validation.

The machine-readable protocol is `results/protocol.json`; its frozen hash is
recorded in `results/protocol_hash.json`. Raw public archives are fetched into
the gitignored `data/_cache/` directory and verified against
`data_manifest/datasets.json`.
