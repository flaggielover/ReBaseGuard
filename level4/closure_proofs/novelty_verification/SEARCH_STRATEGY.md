## Frozen evidence questions

Each family asks whether alarm-triggering observations select the data reused
for a new reference, whether the new reference affects the next cycle, whether
the process repeats, and whether derivative/local stability is analyzed.
Detector-statistic reset, continuous online adaptation, Phase-I estimation,
and change-point parameter estimation are kept distinct from recursive
alarm-selected baseline reuse.

## Frozen follow-up plan

Named follow-ups: `Touboul Brette adaptation map`, `Touboul threshold reset`,
`variable forgetting factor threshold`, `post-selection sequential monitoring`.
For high-risk papers, use exact-title/author queries plus references and cited-
by services. Each is tagged `FOLLOW-UP` or `SNOWBALL` in the manifest.
