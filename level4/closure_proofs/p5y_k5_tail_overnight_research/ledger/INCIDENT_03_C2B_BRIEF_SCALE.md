## What happened

The C2b brief asked the stream to report whether its certified tightness at non-target drifts falls below a percentage
scale. The coordinator had derived that scale from committed cell-308 figures. `streams/C_308/A0X/gen/C2B_ROUTE_SUMMARY.md`
line 49 answers that question. Pairing the stream's tightness with that scale is an estimator of the outcome of the
308 exclusion question.

## Consequences

* That sentence is **not** used in any handover, registry or cross-route text.
* The C2b tightness figures themselves remain non-target facts about the strategy.
* Any future X308 protocol must disclose this incident together with incident 02.
* One `PROXY_EXPOSURE` ledger line was appended.
